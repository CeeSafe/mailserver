# SEARCH differences from Dovecot 2.4.5 (without full-text search) that are understood and kept, for
# pkgs/mimap/tools/search-differential.py: `query | message | tag`, each tag defined by a `= tag: reason` line. The
# tool fails on a difference not listed here, and on a listed one that no longer occurs.
= address: Dovecot's FROM, TO, CC and BCC search its parsed and re-written address list (a missing domain becomes @MISSING_DOMAIN, malformed lists are rebuilt); ours search the field's decoded text, as HEADER does on both (HEADER From "@" agrees with our FROM "@")
= html: ours searches HTML as a reader sees it (mmime drops tags, <style> and <script>, and decodes entities); Dovecot without full-text search searches the HTML source
= embedded-header: BODY: an attached message's header is part of the outer message's body, and ours searches it (as Dovecot's own full-text index does); Dovecot's plain BODY skips every header line at any depth
= message-leaf: Dovecot searches the raw body of message/* leaves other than message/rfc822 (message/partial, delivery-status, feedback-report); mmime reads only text/* leaves
= mime-header: TEXT: Dovecot also searches the MIME header of every part (Content-Type and the like, and the base64 lines of a part with no blank line after its header); ours searches the message's header and attached messages' headers
= untyped: a Content-Type with no subtype (X-ZIP, x-sun-attachment, X-BE2) is invalid, and mmime reads the part as text/plain (RFC 2045 §5.2); Dovecot skips it as not text
= bad-encoding: an unknown Content-Transfer-Encoding (duckduckgo): mmime searches the body as it is; Dovecot skips the part
= two-types: two Content-Type fields: mmime uses the first (text/plain), Dovecot the last (application/octet-stream)
